Phone connection, focus and detection help
  • Both iPhones must appear as separate camera sources. A USB cable alone does not make a phone a webcam. Enable Continuity Camera and use the same Apple Account where required.
  • Desk View is another view of one physical camera; it is excluded from the source list.
  • Place the second phone directly overhead and keep both phones still. Move a textured object after the scene has settled.
  • YOLOE segmentation is configured for cans, bottles, cups, boxes, phones, books, pens, balls, mice, keyboards, scissors and remotes. Other objects use a clearly labelled motion fallback. Labels can be wrong: a can may be labelled as a bottle.
  • Continuous autofocus is requested only when the device exposes it. If it does not, focus remains managed by the phone. Use good light and avoid placing the lens too close.
  • The overhead map is in image coordinates. A metric 3D estimate requires camera intrinsics, lens distortion and world-to-camera poses. Browser sampling does not verify sensor synchronization.
