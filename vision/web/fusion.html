OVERHEAD-ONLY CONTROLS
Add an overhead control
Disks automatically become 2D inputs. You can also assign any overhead track as a horizontal slider, vertical slider or 2D pad. No side-camera match or height is required. Can pivot mode lights W/A/S/D from movement around a saved neutral base. The side skeleton estimates top/base lean; overhead pivot position is approximate. Stick heading is separate from measured surface twist. Small travel reaches full input (3.3% of the camera view). For a can joystick, hold the can upright for one second to learn neutral from your actual camera angle. Then use Learn W and Learn D to teach the camera your directions. This removes a fixed viewing-angle bias; it is a relative control, not measured 3D tilt. Recalibrate after moving the camera or can base. Brief gaps hold 130 ms, then ease to neutral over 170 ms. Raise the overhead camera to capture more table; reset zoom cannot widen a fixed lens. W/A/S/D axes are rotated for this camera mounting; Reverse X/Y can invert directions. A plain round disk needs a visible mark or texture to measure rotation.

Spatial workspace
Drag the box to rotate it, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset. The box shows relative movement: X/Y from overhead, Z from the side. Calibration is required for physical distances and accurate 3D position; perspective and object rotation can affect these estimates.
Move each new object briefly in both views. Compatible objects pair automatically when their motion match is unique. Move similar objects one at a time; ambiguous matches wait. Stationary background objects are excluded at acquisition. Motion matching is a consistency check, not proof of identity.
