OVERHEAD-ONLY CONTROLS

Add an overhead control

Disks automatically become 2D inputs. You can also assign any overhead track as a horizontal slider, vertical slider or 2D pad. No side-camera match or height is required. Can pivot mode lights W/A/S/D from movement around a saved neutral base. The side skeleton estimates top/base lean; overhead pivot position is approximate. Stick heading is separate from measured surface twist. Small travel reaches full input (3.3% of the camera view). Side tilt reaches full forward/back input at 12° from neutral when a unique matching can and stable base are visible. Brief gaps hold 130 ms, then ease to neutral over 170 ms. Raise the overhead camera to capture more table; reset zoom cannot widen a fixed lens. W/A/S/D axes are rotated for this camera mounting; Reverse X/Y can invert directions. A plain round disk needs a visible mark or texture to measure rotation.

RELATIVE X / Y / Z

Spatial workspace

The box shows relative movement: X/Y from overhead, Z from the side. Calibration is required for physical distances and accurate 3D position; perspective and object rotation can affect these estimates.

Move each new object briefly in both views. Compatible objects pair automatically when their motion match is unique. Move similar objects one at a time; ambiguous matches wait. Stationary background objects are excluded at acquisition. Motion matching is a consistency check, not proof of identity.
